The Last of Us

The Last of Us is a 2013 action-adventure game developed by Naughty Dog and published by Sony Computer Entertainment. Players control Joel, a smuggler tasked with escorting a teenage girl named Ellie across a post-apocalyptic United States. The game is set in a world ravaged by a mutant fungal infection that has devastated civilization and turned its victims into cannibalistic creatures.

The game combines elements of survival horror and action gameplay. Players must scavenge for supplies, craft weapons, and use both stealth and combat to survive against infected humans and hostile survivors. The story explores themes of love, loss, and the lengths people go to protect those they care about.

  • Developer: Naughty Dog
  • Publisher: Sony Computer Entertainment
  • Released: June 14, 2013
  • Platform: PlayStation 3, 4, PC
  • Genre: Action-Adventure, Survival Horror
